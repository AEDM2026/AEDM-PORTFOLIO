Project Overview

AE Design & Model Source provided integrated mechanical, electrical, and plumbing design for a community church project in Maryland. The facility combines worship space, fellowship areas, and support rooms — each with distinct comfort, lighting, and plumbing needs coordinated under one cohesive MEP package for local code compliance.

Scope of Work

  • HVAC zoning and equipment selection for sanctuary and ancillary spaces
  • Electrical power, lighting, and audio-visual support infrastructure
  • Plumbing for restrooms, kitchen, and janitor facilities
  • Load calculations, panel schedules, and riser diagrams
  • Multi-discipline coordination with architectural volumes and structure
  • Construction documents for permitting in Maryland

Technical Highlights

Worship spaces often feature high ceilings and large open volumes that challenge HVAC distribution and lighting design. Our team sized systems for variable occupancy and acoustic considerations while routing ductwork and conduit to respect architectural expression. Plumbing layouts grouped back-of-house wet walls to simplify construction and future maintenance for the congregation’s volunteer facilities team.

Outcome

The church leadership and design team received fully coordinated MEP drawings that balanced operational efficiency with the project budget, supporting a successful permit review and construction start.
