Project Overview

AE Design & Model Source provided comprehensive MEP engineering design and BIM coordination for the Moxy Knoxville Hotels development — a boutique hospitality project in downtown Knoxville, Tennessee. Our scope encompassed mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems designed to meet brand standards while satisfying local codes and energy requirements.

Scope of Work

  • HVAC load calculations and ductwork layout for guest rooms and common areas
  • Electrical power distribution, panel schedules, and lighting design
  • Plumbing systems including domestic water, sanitary waste, and storm drainage
  • Multi-discipline BIM coordination using Revit MEP
  • Clash detection and resolution with architectural and structural teams
  • Construction document preparation for permit submission

Technical Highlights

Hotel projects demand tight coordination between MEP systems and interior layouts. Our team worked closely with the design architect to route ductwork, conduits, and piping within ceiling plenums while maintaining guest room aesthetics and operational efficiency. All deliverables were produced in accordance with applicable Tennessee building codes and NFPA standards.

Outcome

The project was delivered on schedule with fully coordinated construction documents, enabling the development team to proceed through permitting and into construction with confidence in system integration.

Project Details

  • Discipline: Full MEP
  • Software: Revit MEP, AutoCAD
  • Standards: NEC, NFPA, IBC
  • Region: Southeast USA
