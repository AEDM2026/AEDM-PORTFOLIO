What We Do

Electrical Design

  • Clash Detection

About AEDM

Your Trusted MEP Outsourcing Partner

Founded in 2021 and headquartered in Washington, DC, AE Design & Model Source specializes in MEP design and BIM services for multifamily and hotel developments across the Northeast and Southeast United States.

Our team of trained mechanical and electrical engineers uses AutoCAD MEP, Revit MEP, and BIM Collaborate Pro to deliver projects on time — helping architects and developers scale capacity without compromising quality.
