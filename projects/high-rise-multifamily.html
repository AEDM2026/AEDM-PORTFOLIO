Project Overview

AE Design & Model Source delivered complete electrical engineering for a high-rise multifamily tower in the Northeast — from initial load analysis through construction documents. The design supports dense residential stacking, amenity levels, and underground parking while meeting NEC and IBC requirements for the local jurisdiction.

Scope of Work

  • Building and unit load calculations per NEC Article 220
  • Main service sizing, switchgear, and meter center layout
  • Vertical riser diagrams and branch circuit design for residential units
  • Panel schedules, one-line diagrams, and equipment schedules
  • Common-area and amenity electrical layouts including lighting and power
  • Coordination with structural cores and MEP vertical shafts

Technical Highlights

High-rise residential projects require careful allocation of electrical demand across multiple dwelling units, shared loads, and life-safety systems. Our team modeled feeder and branch loading to optimize conductor sizing and panel distribution while preserving ceiling space for other trades. Deliverables aligned with IBC egress and fire alarm interface requirements for the building type and occupancy.

Outcome

The client received permit-ready electrical drawings with coordinated risers and schedules, enabling the design team to move forward with confidence in service capacity and unit-level power distribution.
